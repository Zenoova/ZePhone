Your SIP line, on every device.

ZePhone is a softphone that connects to your own PBX or SIP provider. You enter your server and your credentials: there is nothing to sign up for with us.

Download ZePhoneView the source

Made for people who live on the phone

Works with your server

Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, 3CX, a carrier or a hosted PBX: enter the server, port, transport (UDP, TCP or TLS) and credentials. Several accounts can be connected at the same time.

Clear voice

HD codecs (Opus, plus G.722 on macOS) with G.711 as the universal fallback, SRTP encryption, separate volume for what you hear and what the other person hears.

Calls in their own window

On computers, each call opens in a separate window while the keypad, contacts and history stay free. Hold, mute, DTMF keypad and blind transfer are one tap away.

Directory built in

Local contacts, your company's LDAP or Active Directory, Microsoft 365, and the contacts of your device. Callers are shown by name when the number matches one person.

Recordings that stay with you

Answered calls can be recorded automatically. The files are stored on your device only, and the option can be switched off in Settings.

See what the network is doing

Advanced call statistics (jitter, round trip, lost packets, buffers) with a plain-words reading of where a dropout comes from.

Private by design

Passwords live in the system keychain, never in clear text. Server certificates are always verified. Read the privacy policy.

Controlled your way

Global keyboard shortcuts, headset buttons, a command line and a menu bar icon on the desktop; a native navigation bar on Android.

Download

ZePhone runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android. Every version is on the releases page; package managers follow automatically.

macOS

brew tap zenoova/zephone
brew install --cask zephone

Windows

winget install Zenoova.ZePhone
choco install zephone

Linux

Debian and Ubuntu (apt), Fedora and RHEL (dnf), Arch (pacman), Snap and AppImage are available. Commands are in the installation guide.

Mobile

Android on Google Play and iPhone / iPad on the App Store: coming soon.
