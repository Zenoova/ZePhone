Setting up an account

ZePhone connects to a SIP server that you choose. Open Settings → SIP accounts → Add and fill in:

  • SIP server: for example sip.example.com;
  • Port and transport (UDP, TCP or TLS): your provider tells you which;
  • Domain, if it differs from the server;
  • Username, authentication username (if different) and password.

The status changes to Registered when it works. If not, it shows the SIP code returned by the server (401 or 403: wrong credentials; 404: unknown user; 408 or 503: server unreachable). We do not provide SIP accounts: they come from your employer, PBX administrator or a SIP provider.

Common questions

I cannot hear anything, or the other person cannot hear me

Check that ZePhone is allowed to use the microphone in your system settings, then pick the right microphone and output in Settings → Audio. During a call, Advanced statistics (the options menu of the call window) shows whether the problem is on the network or on your device.

I do not receive calls when the app is closed

ZePhone receives calls while it is running. On computers you can keep it running in the menu bar or system tray when the window is closed and start it at login. On Android a notification keeps it alive. On iPhone and iPad, incoming calls ring only while the app is open for now.

Where are my recordings?

In the Recordings tab, stored on your device only. You can turn automatic recording off in Settings → Audio. Depending on your country, recording a call may require the other person's consent.

Reporting a problem

Settings → Network has the SIP trace and the engine log. Please remove passwords and phone numbers you do not want to share before sending them.

Crash reports and statistics

You can turn both off in Settings → General → Diagnostics. See the privacy policy.
