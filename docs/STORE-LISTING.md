# Store listing texts

English texts used for the store listings, so they stay versioned with the app. Limits are the stores' own.

## Google Play (default listing, English)

**App name** (22/30): ZePhone: SIP Softphone

**Short description** (78/80): A SIP softphone for your own PBX or provider. HD voice, directory, no sign-up.

**Full description** (2563/4000):

```
ZePhone turns your phone into a SIP extension. Enter the server, username and password your employer, PBX administrator or SIP provider gave you, and you can place and receive calls on your own line. There is nothing to sign up for with us.

WORKS WITH YOUR SERVER
- Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, 3CX, carriers and hosted PBX: any standard SIP server
- Choose the port and the transport: UDP, TCP or TLS
- Connect several accounts at the same time and call from the one you pick
- Clear registration status with the SIP code when something is wrong

CALLS
- Outgoing calls from the keypad, a contact, the history or any SIP address
- Incoming calls with the caller's name when the number is in your directory
- Hold, mute, keypad (DTMF) and blind transfer during a call
- Loudspeaker button and automatic switch to call mode on your phone

GOOD SOUND
- Opus and G.711 codecs, with SRTP media encryption
- Separate volume for what you hear and what the other person hears
- Advanced call statistics: jitter, round trip, lost packets, with a plain-words reading of where a dropout comes from

DIRECTORY
- Your own contacts, with first name, last name, company and numbers
- Your phone's contacts, read-only and only if you allow it
- Your company's LDAP / Active Directory and Microsoft 365 directories
- Every number is callable in one tap

HISTORY AND RECORDINGS
- Incoming, outgoing, missed and declined calls with real durations, search and filters
- Optional automatic recording of answered calls, stored on your phone only and easy to turn off. Depending on your country you may need the other person's consent.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- SIP passwords are stored in a private, system-protected space, never in clear text
- Server certificates are always verified
- No ads, no advertising identifier, no account with us
- Optional crash reports and anonymous usage statistics: on by default, never include numbers, names or call content, and can be turned off in Settings

GOOD TO KNOW
- ZePhone needs a SIP account from your organization or a provider; it does not sell phone numbers or calling credit.
- A small permanent notification keeps ZePhone running so that your line stays registered. Incoming calls ring while ZePhone is running; there is no push notification service yet.
- The microphone permission is required to talk. Bluetooth headsets and your contacts are optional.

ZePhone is built on the open-source Baresip engine and shares its core with the macOS, Windows, Linux and iPhone versions. Source code: github.com/Zenoova/ZePhone
Support: kellis@zenoova.com
```

## Images

Drawn and rendered by [`store-assets/`](../store-assets/README.md) (`node scripts/render-all.mjs`, then `out/<set>/`). Every screen
is a capture of the real application with invented data, in the light or the dark theme; the titles say what the application does
that a plain SIP phone does not: one directory for the contacts, LDAP and Microsoft 365; statistics explained in plain words; the
voice orb; local recordings; several lines with TLS and SRTP; global shortcuts; separate microphone, speaker and ringtone.

|---|---|---|---|
| App Store, iPhone 6.9" | `out/iphone/` | 1320 × 2868 | `iphone-01-hero` and `iphone-02-hero` (one picture cut in two, with the icon), then `iphone-03` … `iphone-07` |
| App Store, iPad 13" | `out/ipad/` | 2752 × 2064 | `ipad-01-hero` (with the icon) … `ipad-04-accounts` |
| Mac App Store | `out/mac/` | 2880 × 1800 | `mac-01-hero` (with the icon) … `mac-05-audio` |
| Google Play, phone (also Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Aptoide) | `out/android/` | 1080 × 1920 | `android-01-hero` … `android-06-accounts` |
| Google Play, 10" tablet | `out/tablet/` | 2560 × 1600 | `tablet-01-hero` … `tablet-03-stats` |
| Google Play, feature graphic | `out/android/` | 1024 × 500 | `play-feature-graphic-1024x500` |
| Microsoft Store, Snap Store, Linux | `out/desktop/` | 1920 × 1080 | `desktop-01-hero` … `desktop-04-shortcuts` |

The App Store pictures carry the [Icon Composer](../src-tauri/icons/ZePhone.icon) icon (light and dark renditions exported with
`ictool`). The iPhone, iPad and Android pictures leave the desktop dock out: those platforms have their own navigation bar.
Limits not covered yet: the Microsoft Store logos (300 × 300 and others), the Play 512 × 512 icon (Google rounds it itself: it needs
a square, not the Icon Composer rendition), iPad portrait and Play 7" tablet sets.
